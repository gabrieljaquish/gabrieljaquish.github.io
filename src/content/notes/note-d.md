This site has no analytics, no trackers, and no ads. Its pages load nothing from any other site: the policy in [Note C](#note-c) allows no other origin.

This site sets no cookies. The only things it keeps are in your browser's local storage, and they never leave it: the background you pick (`gj.bg`), and any knots you plant on the Wood grain background (`gj.bg.grain.knots`).

This site publishes no phone number, email address, or street address. To get in touch, use [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabrieljaquish/).

The build fails if a page's text shows anything that looks like an email address, a North American phone number, a US amateur call sign, a VIN, or exact coordinates. It also fails if a JPEG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF image still carries EXIF or XMP metadata, which is where a photo keeps its location.
